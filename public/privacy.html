Privacy Policy

CourtSide · Effective 14 September 2026

CourtSide is a social and coaching app for tennis players. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it lives, and what you can do about it. We keep it short because you should actually read it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Health data never appears on your profile and is never shared with other players or coaches.

Who can see what

Your profile, posts, clips, hits, questions, and answers are visible to other CourtSide users. Hits disappear from view after 24 hours. Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. Your AI coach conversations, health data, and account details are visible only to you.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores accounts, posts, messages, and uploaded media on servers in the United States, and handles sign-in.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google". We receive your name and email; we never see your Google password.
  • Anthropic powers the AI coach. When you ask the coach a question, your question and a summary of your tennis profile are sent to Anthropic to generate the reply. Anthropic does not use this to train its models.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web version of the app.

Each of these providers processes data only on our instructions and under their own privacy commitments.

The AI coach is not a doctor

Training, injury, and nutrition content in CourtSide — including anything the AI coach says — is general information, not medical advice. If something hurts, see a professional.

Your choices

  • See or change your data: Settings → Edit profile, or Settings → Account centre.
  • Download everything: Settings → Account centre → Download your data.
  • Clear the AI coach's memory: Settings → Account centre → Coach memory → Clear.
  • Disconnect health services or turn off location: Settings → Health and nutrition; Settings → Location.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account centre → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, posts, messages, media, and coach memory. It cannot be undone.

How long we keep things

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, everything tied to it is deleted. Hits are removed from public view after 24 hours and kept in your private archive until you delete them.

Children

CourtSide is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top is always the current version.
