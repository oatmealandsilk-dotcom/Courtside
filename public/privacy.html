Privacy Policy

CourtSide · Last updated: September 21, 2026

CourtSide is a social and coaching app for tennis players. It is new and still in early access. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it. "We" means the people who run CourtSide. We have tried to write it so anyone can read it.

The short version

  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or tracking tools.
  • Most of what you post can be seen by other people using CourtSide. Direct messages can only be read by the people in the chat.
  • Location is off unless you turn it on, and we never receive or save your exact position.
  • You can download your data and delete your account from inside the app.
  • CourtSide is for people 13 and older. Accounts for 13 to 17 year olds get extra protection.

What we do not collect or do

  • We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not show ads. The app has no advertising tools, and no outside analytics or tracking tools built in. The record of what you watch in the feed stays inside CourtSide and is only used to order your feed.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No health data. The Health and nutrition screen is a preview with sample numbers. Linking a wearable or nutrition app is not available yet, so nothing is collected from one.
  • No payment details. CourtSide does not take payments. The Payment methods screen is a preview with sample entries. It only keeps short labels such as "Apple Pay" with your settings. We never ask for or store card numbers or bank details.
  • We only use your camera, photos and microphone when you choose to take or pick a photo or video. We only receive the ones you post.

Who can see what

Treat anything you post outside direct messages as public. Anyone using CourtSide can see:

  • Your name, handle, photo, bio and city.
  • Your tennis profile, including tournament dates you add. Only add what you are happy for others to read. Injury, schedule and equipment notes you give the coach are private: only you and your AI coach see them.
  • Your posts, clips and hits (unless your account is private), your comments, who liked what, who you follow and who follows you.
  • Your discussion threads, answers, questions to coaches and replies. Votes are stored with your account, so they are not anonymous.
  • Tips you post on the tips board, with your name, for anyone signed in to read and vote on.

Private accounts. If you turn on Private account, only followers you approve can see your posts and hits, and the app only shows your tennis profile to them. Your name, handle, photo, bio, city, discussion threads, answers, questions to coaches, and comments you leave on other people's posts can still be seen by everyone.

Hits show on the rail for 24 hours. After that (or sooner, if you archive one) they move to your archive, which only you can see. When you watch a hit, your view is recorded with your account, and the app shows the person who posted it how many people viewed it. Right now a hit cannot be deleted by itself; it is deleted when you delete your account, or you can ask us to delete it.

Views. For regular posts, we record that you viewed a post once, only so the view count is honest. Nobody can see the list of who viewed.

Only you (and CourtSide) can see: your email address, your date of birth, your saved posts, your settings, who you have muted or blocked, and the reports you send. Other people only see whether an account is a teen account, so the teen rules below can work.

Your handle. If you sign up with Google, your handle starts as the part of your email address before the @. Handles cannot be changed in the app yet, so contact us if you want a different one.

Photos and videos are stored as files in online storage. Each file has a long, hard-to-guess web address, and anyone who has that address can open the file, even if the post is later archived, your account is private, or the post is deleted. Photos and videos can also carry hidden details from your phone, such as where they were taken. CourtSide does not add anything like that to your files, but it may not remove what your phone put there.

Direct messages

  • Messages are stored in our database (the online system where the app keeps its information) so both people can read them on any device. The database only lets the people in a chat read it.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted (locked so that only the people in the chat could ever read them). That means CourtSide's operators could technically read them. We do not read your messages unless we need to look into a report, keep someone safe, or follow the law.
  • If you edit a message you sent, it shows as "Edited". If you unsend it, it is removed for everyone in the chat. If you delete a message, it is hidden only for you; the other person still has it.
  • The app records when each person last read a chat, to show unread counts and whether a message was seen. If you turn off Read receipts in Settings, the people you chat with no longer see "Read" under the messages they send you.
  • Only people a teen follows can start a new chat with a teen.

Location

Location is off unless you turn it on (Settings → Location, or the map on the Discuss tab). Your phone or browser then asks your permission, and it is only used while you are using the app.

  • Your exact position stays on your device. It is used there to center the players map and to pick the nearest city from a list built into the app. We do not send your exact position to our servers or save it.
  • The nearest city name is kept on your device only. It is added to your profile or a post only if you tap "Use my location" to put it there yourself.
  • Other players on the map are placed at a fixed spot near the middle of the city written on their profile. The map never shows anyone's real position.
  • Map pictures come from Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android, and OpenFreeMap (using OpenStreetMap data) on the web. Like any map, the map provider receives the area you are looking at. When Location is on, that area is around you.
  • Weather on the map comes from Open-Meteo. The app sends Open-Meteo the center point of the map, rounded to roughly the nearest kilometer (under a mile). When Location is on, that point is near you; otherwise it is the middle of the city on your profile. No account details are sent with it.

Notifications and alerts

  • In-app notifications (likes, comments, follows, replies and so on) are stored with your account, and only you can see yours. Each one records who did what and a short preview.
  • Push alerts (the ones that pop up on your phone): after you sign in, your phone asks once if CourtSide may send them. If you say yes, we save your phone's push address with your account. When something happens for you, our database sends the alert through Expo's push service (Expo is the company whose tools the app is built with), and Apple or Google delivers it to your phone.
  • An alert contains the other person's name, what they did, and a short preview. Message alerts include the words of the message. So that text passes through Expo and Apple or Google, and can show on your lock screen. You can hide previews in your phone's notification settings.
  • To turn alerts off, use your phone's settings. Settings in the app also has switches for alerts about likes and about coach replies. When you sign out on a phone, that phone stops getting your alerts.

Crash reports

When the app hits an error, it sends a crash report to our own database so we can fix it. A report contains the error message and technical details about where in the app's code it happened, the screen you were on, the app version, the kind of device (iPhone, Android or web) and its system version, and your account ID (a random code that identifies your account) if you are signed in. It does not include your messages, posts, photos or videos. Crash reports are only used to fix bugs and are not shared with anyone else.

Coaching and the AI coach

  • Ask a coach questions are public, like discussion threads. Replies come from accounts CourtSide has marked as coaches.
  • Coaching requests you send from a coach's page are saved with your account, and only you can see them in the app. No payment is taken.
  • The Apply to be a coach form sends us what you enter (your name, email, phone number, ratings, experience, certifications, references and how you coach) and your résumé file if you attach one. Your application and résumé are private: only you can see your own from the app, and only the CourtSide team reviews them. The résumé is kept in private storage, not at a public web address. If you delete your account, your application is deleted with it, but the résumé file itself is not erased automatically yet; email us and we will remove it.
  • The AI coach is not open yet. When it opens, it will be powered by Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. When you ask it something, our server will send Anthropic your question, your recent conversation with the coach, and a summary of your tennis profile: rating, play style, goals, current injury and schedule notes, next tournament, your training plan for the week, and any recovery numbers. To remember what you are working on, we will store your recent conversations with the coach, a short summary of them, and how many questions you asked each day. Anthropic's terms for business customers say it does not use this information to train its AI.
  • Training, injury and nutrition content in CourtSide, including anything the AI coach says, is general information, not medical advice. If something hurts, see a doctor or physio.

Reports, blocking and muting

  • You can report a post, a hit or a profile from its ••• menu. We save who sent the report, who it is about, and what was reported, and a person at CourtSide reviews it. We do not tell the other person who reported them.
  • Blocking someone hides their posts, hits, threads and your chat with them from you. It does not yet stop them from seeing your public posts. If you need that, make your account private too.
  • Muting someone stops their posts from showing up for you. They are not told.

Threads from other websites

To give the Discuss board something to read, it shows headlines, short excerpts, author usernames and links from the public feed of Tennis Warehouse's Talk Tennis forum, and sometimes from Reddit's r/10s and r/tennis. These belong to the people who wrote them and to those websites. Tap one to read it and reply on the original site. CourtSide is not connected to, or approved by, Tennis Warehouse or Reddit. A copy of these headlines is refreshed about every six hours and saved on our website. If that copy cannot be reached, the app may load the feeds straight from those sites, and they will see an ordinary web request from your device. If you wrote a thread and want it taken off CourtSide, email us.

The waitlist and the feedback box

Our waitlist page asks for your email, and your first name if you want to give it, so we can invite you to try the iPhone app before it is in the App Store. If you arrived from a link with a label on it (such as one shared on Reddit or X), we note that label too, so we know which posts are working. The page's feedback box takes your message and, only if you add it, your email so we can reply. Nobody but CourtSide's admins can read either list. We use your email for your invite and the occasional build update, never sell it or hand it to anyone else, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe. To be taken off the list, unsubscribe from any email or email us, and we delete you. You need to be 13 or older to join.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores accounts, posts, messages and uploaded photos and videos, handles sign-in, sends account emails (like confirming your email or resetting your password), and runs our small server programs.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google". We receive your name, email address and basic profile details; we never see your Google password. Google also draws the map on Android phones and delivers alerts to Android phones.
  • Apple draws the map on iPhone and delivers alerts to iPhones.
  • Expo passes push alerts from our database to Apple and Google.
  • GitHub Pages hosts our website: the web version of the app, these policy pages, and the list of Talk Tennis and Reddit headlines the app loads.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather on the map.
  • OpenFreeMap, using OpenStreetMap data, draws the map in the web version.
  • Anthropic will power the AI coach once it opens.

Each one only gets what it needs to do its job, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

What the app keeps on your device

The app saves a few things on your phone or in your browser: your sign-in, so you stay signed in; up to five logins saved for quick account switching (kept in your phone's secure storage, or your browser's storage on the web); a few settings; a short-lived copy of the Talk Tennis and Reddit headlines; and your answer to the age question. If someone under 13 tries to sign up, the device remembers that too (see below). The web version does not use tracking cookies. "Log out of all devices" in Account center signs you out everywhere and forgets the saved login on that device.

Your choices

  • See or change your details: Settings → Edit profile, or Settings → Account center for your email, password and Google sign-in.
  • Make your account private: Settings → Private account.
  • Turn location on or off: Settings → Location.
  • Turn alerts off: your phone's settings, or the alert switches in Settings.
  • Download your data: Settings → Account center → Download your data. You get one file with your profile, posts, hits, comments, threads, answers, who you follow, saved items, your messages and your coaching requests. It covers what the app has loaded, so if something is missing, email us and we will send the rest.
  • Delete something: delete a post from its ••• menu, and unsend or delete a message by holding it. Comments, threads, answers, tips and hits cannot be deleted one by one in the app yet. Email us and we will remove them.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account center → Delete account. Type DELETE and tap "Delete my account". This cannot be undone.
  • Anything else, such as asking what we hold about you or asking us to fix or remove something: email us at the address below.

What deleting your account removes

Deleting your account removes your sign-in, profile, date of birth, posts, hits, comments, likes, saves, follows, follow requests, discussion threads (and the replies on them), answers, questions to coaches (and the replies on them), coaching requests, tips, notifications, the reports you sent, your settings, your push addresses, and anything the AI coach stored about you. Messages you sent are removed from the other person's chat too.

Some things stay:

  • Messages other people sent you stay in their copy of the chat.
  • Reports other people made about you are kept, to keep CourtSide safe.
  • Crash reports are kept, but are no longer linked to your account.
  • Photo and video files you uploaded are not automatically erased yet. The posts are gone from the app, but the files may still open at their web address. Email us and we will erase them.
  • Your old account ID (a random code) may remain in a few places, such as vote tallies or other people's block lists, with nothing else attached to it.
  • Our providers may keep backup copies for a short time before they are replaced.

How long we keep things

We keep your information as long as your account exists, unless you delete it sooner. Hits leave the rail after 24 hours and stay in your private archive. Reports and crash reports are kept as long as we need them to keep CourtSide safe and working.

Children and teens

CourtSide is for people 13 and older. Everyone gives a date of birth once, either on the sign-up form or on a one-time screen the next time they open the app.

  • Under 13: no account is made. If one was just made (for example, with "Continue with Google"), it is deleted right away, and the date of birth is not kept. That phone or browser then remembers the answer and will not offer sign-up again.
  • 13 to 17 (teen accounts): the account starts as private, so only approved followers see posts and hits. The teen can change this later. Only people the teen follows can start a new chat with them. Chats that already exist carry on.
  • Your date of birth is private and never shown on your profile. You cannot change it in the app; if you made a mistake, email us.

If you think a child under 13 has an account, or you are a parent with a question, email us and we will help. We will delete a child's account.

Security

  • Passwords are handled by Supabase's sign-in system and stored in scrambled form. We never see your password.
  • Our database has rules, called row-level security, that decide who can read or change each piece of information. For example, only the people in a chat can read its messages.
  • The app talks to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a problem, please tell us.

Where your information is stored

CourtSide is run from the United States. Our service providers may store and process your information in the United States and other countries.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
