CourtSide · WHOOP integration preview

Tennis sessions from WHOOP, logged and shared on CourtSide

CourtSide is a social app for tennis players. When a member's WHOOP records a workout with the sport Tennis, CourtSide asks them to log it. Logging adds time on court to their private practice log and streak. Sharing the session in a post is a separate, optional step, and heart rate appears on a post only if the member switches it on for that post.

These are design previews of screens in development (October 2026). Numbers shown are examples.

What CourtSide reads

  • read:workout: sport, start and end, average and max heart rate
  • read:recovery, read:sleep, read:cycles: shown only to the member on their private Health page
  • read:profile: to link the right WHOOP account

How it's protected

  • Sessions are private to the member by default
  • Shown to others only through a post the member chooses to publish
  • No advertising use, no selling data, no AI model training
  • Disconnecting deletes the member's WHOOP tokens; deleting the account deletes WHOOP-derived data
