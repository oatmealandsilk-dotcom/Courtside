Screens on this page
--------------------
Every screen image in this folder is an unretouched screenshot of the CourtSide
iPhone app, captured 22 September 2026 on the owner's own phone, from his own
account (@mrdinosaur62). Nothing is a mockup, a render or a composite. They were
resized for the web and re-encoded as JPEG; nothing else was changed.

post.jpg     the feed, a post of photos from a session
feed.jpg     the feed, a clip playing full screen
profile.jpg  the owner's profile and his grid of posts
thread.jpg   a community thread about strings
board.jpg    the community board, threads by topic

A sixth capture (Find Players, with the map) is deliberately not shipped: it
showed another player's name, handle and city.

On the page (Oct 5) each phone fades out toward its foot, in CSS only: the hero
before the post's like and comment counts, and the four in "See it in action"
fully before the tab bar (gone by 78% of the phone's height; the bar starts at
78.5%), which the app has since replaced. The files are unchanged.
