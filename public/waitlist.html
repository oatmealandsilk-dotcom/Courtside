What’s in it

Four things, all built around tennis instead of squeezed into an app made for everything else.

The feed

Live

Full-screen clips and posts from other players, one at a time. Every clip carries the player’s level, so you can read it properly instead of just watching it.

Community

Live

Ask about your serve, your strings or your next tournament, and get answers from players who have been there.

Messages

Live

Private threads with your hitting partners and your training group, without the group chat you already have getting lost.

Coaching

Being built

Real coaches, approved one by one, for video reviews and written answers about your game. This is the set we’re playing now.
